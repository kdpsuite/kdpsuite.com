# KDP blog topics competitors are missing

**Date:** 2026-08-18  
**Genre:** landscape + validation  
**Decision:** which new posts KDP Creator Suite should write because named competitors skip them, and because they feed formatting, coloring conversion, templates, batch ops, royalty/trim tools.

Research only. No posts were written.

---

## Bottom line

The named corpus (Atticus, Vellum, BookBolt, Kindlepreneur, Reedsy, SPS, Written Word Media, ALLi, Jane Friedman) is saturated on **how to publish, keywords, ads, generic hardcover-vs-paperback, and a 5-step coloring marketing guide**. It is empty or thin on **classification, Expanded Distribution kill-rules, the June 2025 sub-$9.99 print royalty cliff, Live Element Margin vs bleed, hardcover trim traps for 8.5×11, and catalog ops under the 3/day cap**.

Do not rewrite Amazon Help. Translate Help into failure modes this product actually prevents.

**Adversarial constraint:** named competitors were **absent from page 1** on 20 spot-checked queries [26](sources/26_serp_fanout_20_queries.md). That is not the same as an empty SERP. KDPPreflight, PrintValidation, KDPEasy, and coloring-book SaaS already own generic rejection, bleed, gutter, spine math, AI checkboxes, and photo-to-coloring. BookBolt already has an A+ guide for low-content [27](sources/27_bookbolt_a_plus_low_content.md). Write only where forums, wrong affiliate math, or Amazon-only tables remain.

## Ranked topics to write

Scoring: demand (forum/policy/search intent), coverage vs named competitors, product fit (1–5), type.

### P0 — write first (white space vs named competitors + high product fit)

**1. Coloring books are not low-content on KDP (and the checkbox that gets you rejected)**  
- Query: `KDP coloring book low content` / `is a coloring book low content KDP`  
- Type: searchable + shareable (contrarian vs BookBolt lumping)  
- Why missing: Amazon Help says coloring/puzzle books are *not generally* low-content [05](sources/05_kdp_low_content.md). BookBolt still markets activity books as low-content [15](sources/15_bookbolt_blog.md). Kindlepreneur coloring post never classifies [09](sources/09_kindlepreneur_coloring_book.md). Wrong checkbox → rejection. Right classification changes ISBN, series, Look Inside, ED, and review time [05](sources/05_kdp_low_content.md) [24](sources/24_kdp_timelines.md).  
- Product: templates + compliance validation. Fit 5.  
- Confidence: high.

**2. Expanded Distribution will not take your coloring book, journal, or heavy-ink interior**  
- Query: `KDP expanded distribution coloring book` / `KDP expanded distribution high ink`  
- Type: searchable  
- Why missing: Help explicitly lists coloring books, lined/blank interiors, high ink concentration, and hardcover as ED-ineligible [06](sources/06_kdp_expanded_distribution.md). D2D also refuses coloring/low-content [23](sources/23_d2d_no_low_content.md). No named-competitor post. 8.25×11 is ineligible for all ED paper/ink combos; 8.5×11 color *is* ED-eligible **except** coloring is banned as content type.  
- Product: royalty/trim tools + honest distribution expectations. Fit 4.  
- Confidence: high.

**3. Why 8.5×11 coloring books fail as KDP hardcovers**  
- Query: `KDP hardcover coloring book` / `8.5x11 hardcover KDP`  
- Type: searchable (problem intent)  
- Why missing: Paperback coloring default is 8.5×11 [09](sources/09_kindlepreneur_coloring_book.md). Hardcover trims are only five sizes, topping at 8.25×11 — not 8.5×11 [10](sources/10_kindlepreneur_hardcover_vs_paperback.md). Community thread: Canva 8.5×11 paperback accepted, hardcover rejected as incompatible; 350-page color hardcover also uneconomic [21](sources/21_kdp_community_hardcover_coloring.md). Wrap/hinge math differs [07](sources/07_kdp_hardcover_cover.md).  
- Product: trim checker + formatting engine. Fit 5.  
- Confidence: high.

**4. Print Previewer passed — Human Review still says “insufficient bleed” (LEM vs bleed)**  
- Query: `KDP insufficient bleed` / `KDP coloring book rejected bleed` / `KDP print previewer inside margin`  
- Type: searchable (problem)  
- Why missing: Amazon documents bleed/margin math [01](sources/01_kdp_trim_bleed_margins.md) [02](sources/02_kdp_fix_formatting.md) but never names **Live Element Margin**. Community: IR/previewer can pass; later email cites insufficient bleed with no page list [20](sources/20_kdp_community_bleed_coloring.md) [28](sources/28_kdp_community_lem_bleed.md).  
- Coverage vs open web: **thin**, not white — KDPPreflight/KDPEasy listicle rejection. Unique angle is IR vs HR + LEM vs bleed + copyright-in-unsafe-zone.  
- Product: smart formatting / trim checker. Fit 5.  
- Confidence: high.

**5. The June 2025 print royalty cliff: $8.99 journals and coloring books now earn 50%**  
- Query: `KDP print royalty 9.99` / `KDP 50 percent royalty paperback` / `KDP minimum list price 2025`  
- Type: searchable + shareable  
- Why missing: Official change Jun 10, 2025 — 60%→50% below $9.99 USD (and other marketplace floors); edits after that date force the new minimum list price; some color print costs dropped [25](sources/25_kdp_print_royalty_cliff_2025.md). Jane Friedman filed it as news. Kindlepreneur has a generic royalty calculator, not a **catalog repricing SOP** for sub-$9.99 print SKUs across marketplaces. SERP for hardcover-vs-paperback royalty is saturated; this cliff query is not owned by named competitors [26](sources/26_serp_fanout_20_queries.md).  
- Product: royalty calculator. Fit 5.  
- Confidence: high.

## Adversarial pass (steel man)

**“Kindlepreneur already owns this.”** They own coloring *as a publishing/marketing walkthrough* and hardcover *as novel format strategy*. They do not own classification, ED bans, or 8.5×11 hardcover failure. Do not attack their 5-step post; write the specs they skipped.

**“Amazon Help is the canonical answer.”** True for numbers. False for publishers who still fail after reading Help (previewer vs email; Canva sizes; coloring lumped with journals). Our job is forensics + tools, not restating tables.

**“Low-content is dead / oversaturated.”** Keyword research and “how to make a journal” are oversaturated (BookBolt). Classification, ED, and coloring-as-not-low-content are not. Also: flooding template clones hits “excessively reused” [04](sources/04_kdp_content_quality.md) — do not publish a spam-scaling guide.

**“No search volume, only Reddit anecdotes.”** Reddit was not captured this run. KDP Community URLs were. Policy pages are themselves demand (publishers land on them). Treat forum-only topics without a Help URL as medium confidence.

**“KDPEasy already did coloring rejections.”** Yes [22](sources/22_adversarial_seo_fillers.md) [26](sources/26_serp_fanout_20_queries.md). Unsourced “90% rejected” marketing. Cite Amazon, add LEM vs bleed, add classification/ED that they still skip.

**“Absence of Kindlepreneur on page 1 means write everything.”** False. Tool SEO already occupies generic rejection, calculators, and photo-to-coloring. Only write where the SERP is forums, wrong math, or Amazon-only tables.

**“A+ for low-content is white space.”** False vs BookBolt [27](sources/27_bookbolt_a_plus_low_content.md). True vs Kindlepreneur.
