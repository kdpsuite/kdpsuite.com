## Verbatim quotes

> "KDP now offers both a 50% or 60% royalty rate on paperbacks sold on Amazon marketplaces where KDP supports paperback distribution. The royalty rate for your title depends on the list price"

> "50% royalty rate for list prices at or below: 9.98 USD … 60% royalty rate for list prices at or above: 9.99 USD"

Marketplace table (verbatim floors): Amazon.com 9.98/9.99 USD; EUR marketplaces 9.98/9.99 EUR; Amazon.co.uk 7.98/7.99 GBP; Amazon.ca 13.98/13.99 CAD; Amazon.com.au 13.98/13.99 AUD; Amazon.co.jp 999/1000 JPY; Amazon.pl 39/40 PLN; Amazon.se **109/110 SEK**.

> "If you enable Expanded Distribution, the royalty is 40% of the book's list price … minus printing costs."

## Notes

Jun 10 2025 Community announcement (source 25) listed 99 SEK. Live Help on 2026-08-21 lists 109 SEK (50%) / 110 SEK (60%). USD/EUR/GBP/CAD/AUD/JPY/PLN floors match the announcement. Help does not name the June 10 2025 effective date. Catalog SOP still absent from Kindlepreneur coloring/low-content articles.
