## Finding

Named competitors (Kindlepreneur, Reedsy, Atticus, BookBolt, SPS, ALLi, Jane Friedman) did **not** appear in top results for any of the 20 queries.

YouTube rarely appeared. Technical queries are article + tool-landing territory.

## White space vs named competitors AND thin on the open web

- KDP coloring book too much ink print — KDP Community + guesses
- A+ content low content books KDP — Amazon Help workaround; Written Word Media general A+
- activity book KDP interior specifications — scattered niche posts
- KDP journal template bleed 6x9 — template shops; at least one affiliate uses **wrong bleed height** (6.125×9.125 vs Amazon 6.125×9.25)
- KDP print preview errors inside margin — forums + KDPPreflight
- KDP account warning “does not provide a reading experience” — Kindle-ebook skewed, not print
- KDP batch upload multiple books — GitHub automation spam, not a 3/day SOP
- KDP content quality guidelines low content 2025/2026 — Amazon + generic how-tos

## Saturated on the open web (do not treat as white space)

Even though named competitors are absent, tool SEO owns:

- manuscript rejected PDF fix (KDPPreflight, ebookpbook, Cambric, BookReady)
- paperback PDF rejected bleed
- coloring DPI/line art specs (coloringbook.dev, KDPEasy, BookIllustrationAI)
- hardcover wrap / spine calculators
- hardcover vs paperback royalty
- gutter by page count (table cloned everywhere)
- AI disclosure checkbox explainers (2026)
- convert photo to coloring book (SaaS landings)
- trim size mismatch (PrintValidation)
- ED printing cost (misconception already answered: print cost same, royalty 40%)

## Notes

Absence of Kindlepreneur on page 1 is an opportunity **only** where the SERP is forums, stale Amazon PDFs (2018 print guidelines), or wrong affiliate math — not where KDPPreflight/PrintValidation/KDPEasy already ship dedicated 2026 guides.
