# Source 23 — Draft2Digital: will not distribute low-content / coloring

- URL: https://draft2digital.com/knowledge-base/
- Type: industry (adjacent distributor policy)
- Retrieved: 2026-08-18
- Credibility: 4
- Recency: 4
- Bias: D2D catalog quality
- Used for: H3, wide-distribution topic

## Verbatim quotes

> "Draft2Digital will not publish or distribute books considered low-content. We do not accept content that is excessively reused, recycled, or repeated within or across books."

Low-content includes: "Books containing a large number of blank or lined/dot/grid pages", "Coloring books"

> "Because this type of book is difficult to fill out digitally [...] Draft2Digital will not distribute journals, workbooks, planners, calendars, or content that is primarily interactive"

## Notes

Wide distribution is not a default for this ICP. Pair with Amazon ED exclusions (source 06). Named competitor blogs still pitch “publish wide” generically.
