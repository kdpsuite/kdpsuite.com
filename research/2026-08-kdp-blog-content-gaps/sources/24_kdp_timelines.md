# Source 24 — KDP Help: Timelines (low-content 10 business days)

- URL: https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G202173620
- Type: primary
- Retrieved: 2026-08-18
- Credibility: 5
- Recency: 5
- Bias: Amazon estimates
- Used for: H3 catalog ops

## Verbatim quotes

> "Once you've submitted your book or update for publication, it takes 3 business days for your book (or update) to be Live on Amazon. For low-content books, such as journals or notebooks, it can take up to 10 business days."

> "Updates to my paperback or hardcover manuscript appear? Up to 72 hours. For low-content books, it can take up to 10 business days."

## Notes

Coloring books, if correctly NOT marked low-content, should use the 72-hour clock; journals use 10 days. Misclassification both rejects (source 05) and wrecks launch calendars. No named-competitor post makes this operational.
