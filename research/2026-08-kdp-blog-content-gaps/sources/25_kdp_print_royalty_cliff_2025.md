## Verbatim quotes

> "Following our announcement on May 13, and for the first time since starting print operations almost two decades ago, we’ve changed the royalty rate for print books priced below certain list prices (e.g. less than 9.99 USD) from 60% to 50%."

> "Starting on June 10, 2025, we’ve reduced the royalty rate for print books from 60% to 50% for books priced below: 9.99 USD, 9.99 EUR, 7.99 GBP, 13.99 CAD, 13.99 AUD, 99 SEK, 40 PLN, and 1000 JPY. Books with list prices at or above the amounts listed will continue to earn a 60% royalty rate."

> "If you make updates to book details or content after June 10, 2025, you will be required to update your list price to reflect the new minimum list price."

> "Starting on June 10, 2025, we’ve reduced printing costs for [...] All regular and large trim size paperbacks printed in standard color and purchased from Amazon.com."
