## Verbatim quotes

> "A low-content book has minimal or no content on the interior pages. Low-content books are generally repetitive, and designed to be filled in by the user. This does not typically include activity books, such as puzzle books or coloring books, which generally do not feature repetitive content on each page."

Table: Low-content = notebooks, planners, diaries/journals, prompt journals, log books. Not generally low-content = novels, puzzle books, **coloring books**, photography, children's books.

> "Under Categories you need to check the Low-content box. Please note that if you do not select this box, or select a different box, your book will be rejected."

> "Expanded Distribution is not supported for low-content books, regardless of the ISBN option you select."

> "The sample and the back cover thumbnail are currently not supported for low-content books published without ISBN. [...] Alternatively, you can use A+ Content to add images of your book's interior"

> "Low-content books are not eligible for creating a series, regardless of the ISBN option you select."

> "Low-content books are not eligible for the free KDP ISBN."

## Notes

Amazon explicitly splits coloring books off low-content. BookBolt and most “low content KDP” blogs lump them together. Consequences (ISBN, series, Look Inside, ED, 10-day review, rejection if box is wrong) are not explained as a dedicated competitor article in the named corpus.
