# Source 04 — KDP Help: Guide to Kindle Content Quality

- Type: primary (official policy)
- Retrieved: 2026-08-18
- Credibility: 5
- Recency: 5
- Bias: Amazon; eBook-weighted but cited from print Content Guidelines
- Used for: H3, catalog-quality topic

## Verbatim quotes

> "We do not allow content that disappoints our customers or creates a poor shopping experience, including but not limited to:"

> "Content that does not provide an enjoyable reading experience"

> "Content that is excessively reused, recycled, or repeated within or across books"

> "Content that is rebranded or re-released with significant changes to the metadata, resulting in a confusing reader experience"

> "Companion guides (including summaries, study guides, workbooks, analyses, and other related content based on other books) are not allowed, with limited exceptions for guides with positive reader engagement."

## Notes

This is the policy language forums paraphrase as “does not provide a reading experience.” Amazon does not publish a coloring/journal-specific explainer. High-volume template catalogs collide with “excessively reused.” Activity books that are workbooks *based on another book* collide with companion-guide rules; original activity books do not — that distinction is a topic gap.
