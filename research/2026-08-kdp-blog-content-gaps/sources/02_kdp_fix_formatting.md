## Verbatim quotes

> "To make sure your book meets our quality standards, we review it in two ways: Automated check. When you set up your book and upload your file, our Print Previewer tool check for errors you need to fix before you can submit for publication."

> "If your book has bleed, add 0.125\" (3.2 mm) to the width and 0.25\" (6.4 mm) to the height. For example, if your trim size is 6\" x 9\" (15.24 x 22.86 cm), set the page size to 6.125\" wide x 9.25\" high"

> "If your pages have a background color or graphic and Print Previewer is showing a margin error, first try changing the Bleed Settings option to “Bleed”"
