## Verbatim quotes

> "Once you have a print-ready file, uploading a coloring book to Amazon isn’t all that different from uploading any other paperback. Where people tend to get into trouble is everything that happens before that."

> "Use AI-generated images" listed as option 3 for art, with caveat: "if the alternative is not making the book at all, AI might be a helpful first step."

> "if you are using full bleed images through Atticus, there’s a handy tool that will tell you exactly how many pixels your image should be"

> "the most common [sizes] are 8.5 x 11 inches for most coloring books"

> "You will at least need 24 pages to be able to use print-on-demand software. [...] each page can have a blank page behind it"

No mention of: Expanded Distribution coloring exclusion; low-content vs coloring classification; Print Previewer bleed mismatch; hardcover trim incompatibility with 8.5x11; AI disclosure checkbox; high-ink ED ban; 300 DPI as a rejection mode.
